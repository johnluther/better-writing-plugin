# Tells: what to hunt for in the pass

Read the draft one sentence at a time with this file open. The structural
tells come first because they survive any word swap. The word list is last
because it is the easiest part and the least important.

## Structural tells

| Tell | What it looks like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Announcing | "Here is why." "Here is what works for me." "In this post". "Let me explain." "Let's dive in." "I want to share". | Delete the sentence. Start with the point it announced. |
| Portable sentence | Could be moved into a different article unchanged. "Distractions are everywhere." "Small teams win by staying simple." | Add the particular it stands in for, or cut it. |
| Invented episode | A scene from the author's life the author never mentioned: the spare bedroom, the dog, the new hire who asks the same question every few months. | Replace with a bracketed ask: [AUTHOR: ...]. |
| Vague source | "Studies have shown". "From what I've read". "Experts agree". "Research suggests". | Name the study, the number and the year, or cut it. |
| Uniform rhythm | Every sentence 6 to 12 words. Reads as confident and says nothing. | One sentence past 25 words per paragraph, one under 8. |
| Balanced pair | "I never fully worked and I never fully rested." "Not my leftovers." "Freedom without structure gives you scattered days." | One per piece at most. Usually zero. |
| Not X but Y | "It's not just about managing time, it's about managing energy." "This isn't a trick. It's a system." | State Y. Drop the frame. |
| Decorative triplet | "the right systems, the right tools, and the right mindset". "pipelines, monitoring and someone who cares". | Two items, or the one that matters, or a real list of four with specifics. |
| Adjective as fact | "significant", "meaningful", "transformative", "truly", "actually", "genuinely", "real". | The number, the event or the name the adjective was covering for. |
| Listicle | Numbered headers over a 500-word post. "Five rules that". | Paragraphs that argue one claim. Headers only past 800 words or on request. |
| The bow | A closing paragraph that restates the thesis, offers an aphorism ("a marathon, not a sprint", "you need a better setup"), says "remember", or asks the reader to comment. | End on the last point or a fact. |
| Engagement bait | "What is your best trick? Let me know." "Share your thoughts below." | Delete. |
| Hollow hedge | "It could be argued". "Some might say". "In many cases". | Say what you doubt and why, or state the claim flat. |
| Composite "I" | First person used for a type ("as founders, we all know the feeling"). | "I" only on what the author did or thinks. |
| Sincerity marker | "honestly", "to be clear", "let's be real", "the truth is". | Delete. The sentence that follows is the honest one. |
| Keynote cadence | Short. Lines. For effect. Each on its own paragraph. | Ordinary sentence structure. |

## Word swaps

The easy part. A swap on its own does not fix a sentence; a sentence that
needed a swap usually also needs a particular.

| Avoid | Use instead |
|---|---|
| delve | look into, dig into |
| tapestry | mix, how it fits together |
| in today's fast-paced world, in an era of, in an age of, in the ever-evolving | start with the point |
| in conclusion, in summary, in essence, overall | make the point directly |
| it's important to note, it's worth noting, it cannot be overstated | say it |
| moreover, furthermore, additionally | also, and, plus, or nothing |
| cutting-edge | new, latest |
| revolutionary, revolutionize, game-changing | say what changed |
| transformative | say what it changed into |
| leverage, utilize, harness the power of | use |
| multifaceted, nuanced | say which facets |
| testament to | sign of, proof of |
| robust, seamless, seamlessly integrated | solid, works with |
| unlock (potential, productivity) | say what it does |
| empower, empowering | help, let |
| navigate the complexities of | deal with |
| at the forefront of | leading in, known for |
| pivotal, crucial, critical (as emphasis) | key, or the reason it matters |
| realm, landscape, space, ecosystem | field, industry, or the actual place |
| symphony, beacon, journey | the plain noun |
| plethora, multitude, myriad | many, or the number |
| imagine a world where | the real situation |
| the X of Y (the power of habit, the art of focus) | say what you mean |
| indeed | cut |
| fostering, cultivating | building, growing, or the verb for what happened |
| holistic approach | say the parts |
| best practices | what works, or the practice itself |
| deep dive, unpack | look at |
| elevate | improve, or say how |
| impactful | say the effect |
| is/are what x sounds like | is/are the result of the condition |
